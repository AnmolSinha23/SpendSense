DBMS Viva Explanation: How this page works

This report is powered directly by view_monthly_category_summary, a relational VIEW that aggregates the base tables transactions, categories, and users using INNER JOIN, GROUP BY (user_id, year, month, category_id), and aggregate functions: SUM(amount), COUNT(transaction_id), and AVG(amount). No ORM computations are performed in Python.
